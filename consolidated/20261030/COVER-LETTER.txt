Subject: [RFC PATCH 00/29] Apple M1 USB4 PCIe storage, dock display and retained-power suspend

This downstream series combines the measured native PCIe storage path,
Test128 dock display/audio lifecycle, and automatic retained-power s2idle
coordination. Patches 1–26 are unchanged from usb4sd2. Patches 27–29 retain
active PHY/ACIO domains, coordinate NHI control DMA around DART system PM,
and document validation. No user-selected diagnostic suspend mode is needed.

Base: UbuntuAsahi/linux f7734f4c91c0f47d9e762786968057bec99a0788.
The full series is replayed against that actual Git commit, with preimage,
patch and final-content checks. The older sparse reconstruction is provenance,
not a replacement upstream history. See validation/usb4sd3-replay.json.

The ordinary NVMe driver and firmware are unchanged. Experimental AMDGPU,
TTM, Radeon audio, UART/watchdog and reset-ACK classification changes are
excluded. Existing measured storage policy remains in config/. DCP audio
remains opt-in through appledrm.hdmi_audio=1.

Tests154–157 used matched diagnostic modules with the new PM logic: late/noirq
rehearsal, real s2idle with power-button wake, release of retained power on
ACIO shutdown/start, and real s2idle with asynchronous device PM. HDMI and
fixed-range hashes of TB3 NVMe, USB/UAS storage and SD passed. These results
do not replace a combined-kernel boot. The previous 26-patch full build passed
compilation/module checks; the new 29-patch full build and hardware regression
are tracked separately.

Active-link hibernation/deeper sleep and restoration after full controller
power loss remain unsupported. Active dock-display sleep, native USB4 NVMe,
device changes during sleep, post-resume writes and power draw need further
validation. The known DCP PMU diagnostic is not declared fixed.

Original authors and trailers of unchanged imported work are preserved.
References are retained under provenance/. Local adaptations and consolidation
are AI-assisted; upstream endorsement and DCO signoffs are not fabricated.
Diagnostic names/interfaces, bindings, style and submission readiness remain
review work. No upstream PR is created by this build workflow.
